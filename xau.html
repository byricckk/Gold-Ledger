The XAU Journal | Day 01 — Present

Let me document this journey from the beginning.

It all started on 26th May when my friend SolomonFX introduced me to the idea of trading. At the time, I honestly didn’t know what I was getting myself into. I just knew I wanted to learn how money could be made from the markets. The next day, I opened my trading account and started looking for capital. I found KSh 1,500 from my rent and deposited it into Exness. That first day didn't go well. I lost the account within minutes. And instead of stopping and asking myself what I had done wrong, I deposited again. It was basically revenge trading. I was trying to recover what I'd just lost instead of understanding why I had lost it. I lost around another $5, and eventually I couldn't even open another position because of the minimum margin required for the way I was trading. That was how my first day ended. The following day, I deposited again. I kept trading. Sometimes I made profits. Sometimes I lost. But I didn't really have a system. I was entering trades without truly understanding the market, and eventually I was left with nothing. I blew the account. At that point, I paused trading and went back to school. A few months later, something changed. I decided that if I was going to trade, I couldn't keep treating it like gambling. I needed to actually learn the market. So I started studying on my own. I began learning about XAUUSD, price action, liquidity, entries, risk management, market structure and everything I could get my hands on. Eventually, I started taking trades based on what I was learning. One day, I opened a position completely on my own and made some profit. The next day, I did it again. For the first time, I started realizing that maybe this wasn't about being lucky. Maybe I could actually learn how this market moves. But the market reminded me very quickly that I still had a lot to learn. One day, I couldn't find an entry throughout the entire day. Then, around midnight, I saw what I thought was an opportunity. I entered. Instead of closing the trade, I decided to leave it running overnight. When I woke up, the position was deep in loss. Eventually, that account was blown too. I deposited again that same day. But this time, I went back to looking for actual setups instead of trying to force the market to give me money. I found some entries and started floating in profit. Then came 2nd October. I found a setup similar to what Lichman had been looking for, and we entered. This time, things went well. Before NFP, I closed the position in good profit around $500+. Then NFP came. And, of course, I couldn't just sit there and watch. 😂 I entered again. The trade started floating in profit, and once again I was right in the middle of the thing that had been destroying me from the beginning: emotion. Because trading isn't just about finding an entry. It's about what you do after you enter. It's about whether you can stay disciplined when you're winning. Whether you can accept a loss without revenge trading. Whether you can walk away when there is no setup. Whether you can protect your capital after making money. And most importantly, whether you can repeat the same process tomorrow. Looking back, I've already blown accounts, revenge-traded, overtraded, held trades overnight, chased entries, and made decisions based on emotion. But every one of those mistakes taught me something. I'm not documenting this journey because I've already made it. I'm documenting it because I'm still becoming the trader I want to be. I don't know where this journey will take me. Maybe there will be more losses. Maybe there will be more wins. Maybe I'll make mistakes that I haven't even made yet. But one thing is different now. I'm no longer just trying to make money from XAU. I'm trying to understand it.
